Introduction to Vocaloid

It all started as a voice synthesizer software that was initially produced and developed through a collaborative project between Yamaha Corporation and Pompeu Fabra University.

It allows independent music producers to release fully produced vocal tracks without the need to have a human singing in front of a microphone.

Synthesizer Software & Interface

Music producers can synthesize the voicebanks of the software by typing out the lyrics and inputting a melody into a piano-roll type interface. The software engine selects appropriate vocal fragments (sampled from real voice actors or singers) from a "singer library" and concatenates them to fit the pitch and timing of the song.

A Singer in a Box

These voicebanks are often sold as “a singer in a box” and marketed with unique character avatars (such as MEIKO, KAITO, and the globally recognized Hatsune Miku).

Live Concert Projections

Some of these virtual idols have even transcended software, performing at sold-out live concerts as stunning on-stage holographic projections for global audiences.
