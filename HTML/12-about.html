Meet the Team

Track: Killing In The Name

Artist: Rage Against The Machine

Published: November 2, 1992

It was my first exposure to rock/metal as a genre. The song itself is a protest to police brutality, institutional racism, and the abuse of state power in the US. I listen to the song whenever I need to remind myself of my morals, what I have to fight for, and simply because I enjoy the lyrics. Especially the last verse.

Track: See You // 有天會再相見 （yǒu tiān huì zài xiāng jiàn）

Artist: DIOR 大颖

Published: September 2, 2024

I like "See You" because it is emotional while also giving hope that people will meet again someday. The lyrics are meaningful, and I like its calm, gentle rhythm. It's not rock or R&B, which suits my preference for soft Mandopop songs.

Track: Masshiro na Yuki // まっしろな雪

Artist: Halozy

Published: December 30, 2015

I like this song because of how the melody sounds throughout and it feels vibrant to listen to, overall even tho the lyrics are in japanese it feels like taking a breather while listening to the song.

Track: The First Snow

Artist: EXO

Published: December 9, 2013

❄️ I love this song because I love snow, and winter usually falls around December. ✨ December is my favourite month too — it feels magical, peaceful, and full of memories.

Our Story

Moreover, music is a universal form of expression that connects people from different backgrounds and brings together those who share a common interest in music.
