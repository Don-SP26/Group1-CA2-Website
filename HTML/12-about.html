Who We Are

Four songs, four reasons, and a small preview of the music that inspired us to build this site.

Team picks

Track: Killing In The Name

Rage Against The Machine

November 2, 1992

It was my first exposure to rock/metal as a genre. The song itself is a protest to police brutality, institutional racism, and the abuse of state power in the US. I listen to the song whenever I need to remind myself of my morals, what I have to fight for, and simply because I enjoy the lyrics. Especially the last verse.

Track: See You // 有天會再相見（yǒu tiān huì zài xiāng jiàn）

DIOR 大颖（dà yǐng）

September 2, 2024

I like "See You" because it is emotional while also giving hope that people will meet again someday. The lyrics are meaningful, and I like its calm, gentle rhythm. It's not rock or R&B, which suits my preference for soft Mandopop songs.

Track: Masshiro na Yuki // まっしろな雪

Halozy

December 30, 2015

I like this song because of how the melody sounds throughout and it feels vibrant to listen to, overall even though the lyrics are in Japanese it feels like taking a breather while listening to the song.

Track: The First Snow

EXO

December 9, 2013

I love this song because it reminds me of snow and the beauty of winter, especially in December, which is my favorite month as it feels magical, peaceful, and full of memories.

Our Story

We chose this topic because we enjoy listening to different types of music and discovering new artists and albums. Music plays an important role in people's life as it provides entertainment, helps people relieve stress, and helps to create meaningful experiences. Moreover, music is a universal form of expression that connects people from different backgrounds and brings together those who share a common interest in music.
