Renowned Music Videos

Iconic music videos that changed the music industry.

Thriller

Michael Jackson

Released: 1983

Pop Horror

⭐ Why It's Iconic

Thriller changed the way people viewed music videos. Instead of a simple performance, it told a cinematic horror story complete with professional acting, choreography, costumes, and special effects.

🏆 Awards

  • MTV Video Music Awards
  • Grammy Hall of Fame
  • Library of Congress National Film Registry

🌍 Cultural Impact

  • Popularised cinematic music videos
  • Famous zombie dance worldwide
  • Inspired countless artists and filmmakers
  • Still celebrated every Halloween

🎬 Behind The Scenes

Thriller was directed by John Landis and took several days to film. The production used professional movie makeup artists, elaborate costumes, and a large team of dancers. With a budget of around $900,000, it was one of the most expensive music videos ever made at that time.

Rhythm Nation

Janet Jackson

Released: 1989

Pop Dance

⭐ Why It's Iconic

Rhythm Nation is one of Janet Jackson’s most famous videos. Its bold black-and-white style, powerful choreography, and social message made it a standout pop classic.

🏆 Awards

  • MTV Video Music Award recognition for choreography
  • Grammy-winning Rhythm Nation 1814 era
  • One of Janet Jackson’s most celebrated music videos

🌍 Cultural Impact

  • Influenced pop music videos with its cinematic style
  • Made Janet Jackson a major visual icon in music
  • Still remembered for its choreography and social message

🎬 Behind The Scenes

The video was filmed in black and white in a warehouse-like setting to match its serious tone and message of unity.
